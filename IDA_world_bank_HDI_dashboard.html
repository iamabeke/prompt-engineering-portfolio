3 Actions IDA Leaders Must Take Now

ACTION 01

Infant mortality is the single biggest threat to life expectancy

Infant mortality has a –0.94 correlation with life expectancy — the strongest of any factor. Low income countries average 106 infant deaths per 1,000 births vs 40 in upper-middle income countries.

→ Prioritise maternal & infant health funding

ACTION 02

Sub-Saharan Africa & South Asia are rising fast — but still lag critically

SSA and SA recorded the highest HDI growth in the 21st century (+24.5% and +24%). But both regions still sit well below the global average. Progress is real — but fragile without continued investment.

→ Increase IDA allocation to SSA & South Asia

ACTION 03

Internet access & electricity are the fastest routes to closing the income gap

Internet access (0.60 correlation) and electricity (0.54) outperform GDP per capita (0.52) as predictors of life expectancy. Infrastructure investment delivers outsized development returns.

→ Fund digital & energy infrastructure in Low Income nations

3 Insights for the Next IDA Budget Cycle

BUDGET PRIORITY 01

Infant mortality is killing life expectancy — and it's preventable

It's the single strongest predictor of life expectancy across all 190+ countries. Low income nations still average 106 infant deaths per 1,000 births — vs 41 in upper-middle income countries.

📌 Data point: Infant mortality correlation with life expectancy = –0.939 — the highest of any factor in the dataset.

→ Fund maternal & infant health programmes

BUDGET PRIORITY 02

Sub-Saharan Africa is making real progress — but needs sustained support to hold it

SSA recorded the highest HDI growth of any region in the 21st century. But the 2020 COVID dip shows how fragile these gains are without structural investment.

📌 Data point: SSA HDI rose from 0.437 in 2000 → 0.543 in 2021 — a 24.5% improvement, highest globally.

→ Increase IDA allocation to SSA

BUDGET PRIORITY 03

Internet access delivers more development impact than GDP alone

Internet access (0.60 correlation) predicts life expectancy more strongly than GDP per capita (0.52). Low income countries average just 3% internet access vs 19% in upper-middle income nations.

📌 Data point: A 6× internet access gap exists between Low and Upper-Middle income countries (3% vs 19%).

→ Invest in digital infrastructure in low income nations
